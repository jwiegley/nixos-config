networking.firewall opens 17 TCP (22,53,80,139,443,445,465,587,853,993,1883,2222,4190,5357,5432,8096,21063)
and 6 UDP (53,137,138,3702,5353,5540) with trustedInterfaces=[lo] only.
NixOS emits allowedTCPPorts/allowedUDPPorts rules WITHOUT any '-i <iface>' filter
(firewall-iptables.nix:161-165,191-196), so they apply to every present and future interface.

Consequence: the moment any new interface appears (tailscale0, a VPN, a bridge), SMB 445, PostgreSQL 5432,
DNS 53, every nginx vhost on 80/443 and MQTT 1883 become reachable from it.
This is a latent property of the host, independent of any Tailscale decision, and worth scoping the
global port list to end0/wlp1s0f0 explicitly.
